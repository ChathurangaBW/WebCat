---
name: passive-mapping
description: Build an evidence-backed attack-surface inventory from captured traffic and sitemap data
whenToUse: At the beginning of every engagement and whenever coverage is incomplete
---

1. Enumerate hosts, schemes, ports, paths, methods, parameters, content types, roles, and state-changing operations from passive sources.
2. Attach evidence identifiers to every material claim.
3. Separate directly observed endpoints from inferred or untested endpoints.
4. Record coverage gaps as hypotheses or limitations.
5. Do not convert an anomaly into a finding without a controlled test and independent validation.
