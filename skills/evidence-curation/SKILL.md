---
name: evidence-curation
description: Normalize evidence, preserve provenance, redact secrets, and identify missing controls
whenToUse: Before validation, critic review, and report generation
---

For each claim, identify the exact evidence records that support it. Preserve the distinction between raw observation and interpretation. Verify that secrets are redacted, timestamps and targets are clear, and control evidence exists. Mark unsupported claims as requiring more evidence rather than filling gaps with assumptions.
