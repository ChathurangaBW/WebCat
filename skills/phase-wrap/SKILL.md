---
name: phase-wrap
description: Close a phase with evidence settlement, coverage accounting, and next-step decisions
whenToUse: At every workflow phase transition
---

Summarize completed lanes, evidence created, hypotheses opened or settled, candidate findings, blocked work, and remaining coverage. A phase is not complete until every assigned lane has a result or a documented blocker. Preserve unresolved work in session state rather than silently dropping it.
